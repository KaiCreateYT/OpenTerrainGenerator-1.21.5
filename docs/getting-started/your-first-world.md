# Your First World

This guide walks you through creating your first OTG world using the built-in DefaultPreset.

## Creating a World

1. Launch Minecraft 1.21.1 with OTG installed
2. Click **Singleplayer** → **Create New World**
3. In the **World Type** selector, choose one of the installed OTG presets (e.g. **Default Preset**)
4. Click **Create New World**

OTG registers world types at startup — every DimensionPreset and every `.yaml` file in `WorldPresets/` appears as a selectable world type in the GUI.

## What DefaultPreset Gives You

The built-in **DefaultPreset** produces terrain similar to vanilla Minecraft, but fully configurable:

- Biome distribution controlled by OTG (not vanilla noise)
- Terrain height and shape defined in `DimensionPresetConfig.ini`
- Per-biome settings in `.bc` files under `Biomes/`
- Caves and ravines generated by OTG's carvers
- Vanilla structures (villages, strongholds, etc.) still spawn normally

The `Default.yaml` WorldPreset uses DefaultPreset for the overworld and lets vanilla handle the Nether and End.

## Exploring Your World

Once in-game, check a few things to confirm OTG is working:

- **Biome variety** — travel around and check biomes with F3. OTG biomes show as `otg:biome_name`
- **Terrain shape** — hills, valleys, and coastlines should look smooth and natural
- **Caves** — dig underground. OTG generates its own cave systems alongside vanilla aquifer caves
- **Structures** — villages, temples, and other vanilla structures should appear in appropriate biomes

## Using a Different Preset

To use a custom DimensionPreset (e.g. a community-made one):

1. Place the preset folder in `.minecraft/config/OpenTerrainGenerator/DimensionPresets/`
2. Restart Minecraft — the preset will appear as a new world type in the selector

Each DimensionPreset is a template for a single dimension — it defines terrain, biomes, caves, and portal configuration (block type, color). If you have multiple DimensionPresets installed, you can access additional dimensions in-game through their portals.

For modpack creators who want to ship a predefined world setup — specific dimensions, customized GameRules — see [WorldPreset YAML](../config/world-presets.md).
